Guti Strategy Rulebook

1

The 12 Guti Board

Played on a 5×5 intersection matrix (forming 4×4 internal squares) with horizontal, vertical, and cross-diagonal lines (Alquerque pattern). 25 total intersecting nodes.

2

Initial Setup

Each player starts with 12 pieces: Player 1 occupies rows 1 & 2 plus left 2 points of row 3. Player 2 occupies bottom rows and right 2 points of row 3. The dead center node is empty.

3

Moving & Jumping

Slide 1 step along any drawn line to an empty adjacent node. To capture, jump straight over an opponent piece onto an empty node immediately beyond.

4

Victory Conditions

Win by capturing all 12 enemy pieces, or by completely trapping / blocking the opponent so they have no legal moves left!

1

The Expanded Board

Features a central 5×5 main grid connected to triangular extensions on the top and bottom sides. Total of 37 intersection points.

2

16 Pieces Each

Player 1 starts on top triangle + top 2 grid rows (16 pieces). Player 2 starts on bottom triangle + bottom 2 grid rows (16 pieces). The entire middle row (5 nodes) starts empty.

3

Capturing Battles

Orthogonal and diagonal jump captures are permitted wherever straight drawn lines connect. Captured pieces are removed from the board immediately.

4

Win Condition

Capture all 16 enemy soldiers, or trap their remaining forces so no legal move is possible.

⚡ Pro Tactics & Mechanics

  • Multi-Capture Chain Jumps: If your piece executes a capture jump and lands in a position where another capture is available, you may continue jumping in the same turn!
  • Voluntary End Turn: During a chain jump, you can press End Turn if you prefer not to make further risky jumps.
  • Center Grid Supremacy: Central nodes have 8 connecting lines, granting maximum mobility and attack angles.
  • Flanking & Fortresses: Guard your back lines; keeping pieces clustered in pairs prevents opposing jump landing spots.
