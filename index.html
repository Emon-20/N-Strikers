SHOLO GUTI · 16 SOLDIERS

An Ancient Game of Strategy & Tactics

A traditional South Asian board game

How to Play Sholo Guti

1

Setup

Each player starts with 16 pieces placed on the intersections of their half of the board. The board has a central grid with triangular extensions on two opposite sides.

2

Movement

Players take turns moving one piece along a drawn line to an adjacent empty intersection. Movement can be forward, backward, sideways, or diagonal — as long as there's a connecting line.

3

Capturing

Capture an opponent's piece by jumping over it along a line to an empty space beyond. Multiple captures (chain jumps) in a single turn are allowed!

4

Winning

Win by capturing all 16 of your opponent's pieces, or by blocking them so they have no legal moves left.

💡 Strategy Tips

  • Control the center of the board for more movement options.
  • Look for chain jump opportunities — they can turn the tide!
  • Don't leave your pieces isolated; keep them supported.
  • Force your opponent into positions where their pieces block each other.
